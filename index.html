Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 February 2026

Infinity Games ("we", "us", "our") develops and publishes mobile games. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

This policy applies to all games published by Infinity Games, including Astro Miner.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to play our games. However, our games may automatically collect limited technical and usage data:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Gameplay data: in-game events such as levels completed, items purchased, and session duration. This data is collected in aggregate to help us improve game balance and user experience.
  • Advertising identifiers: your device's advertising ID (e.g. Google Advertising ID) may be collected by our advertising partners to serve relevant ads.
  • Crash and performance data: technical logs that help us identify and fix bugs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve our games.
  • Analyse gameplay patterns to improve game balance and design.
  • Display advertisements within our games.
  • Diagnose technical issues and fix bugs.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Our games use the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy governing the data it collects:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (analytics): Firebase Privacy
  • GameAnalytics (analytics): GameAnalytics Privacy Policy

These services may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage data as described in their respective privacy policies.

4. Advertising

Our games display ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising identifier and device information to serve personalised ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalisation
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy > Tracking > disable Allow Apps to Request to Track

5. Children's Privacy

Our games are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

6. Data Retention

Gameplay analytics data is retained in aggregate form. We do not store data that directly identifies individual users. Third-party services retain data according to their own policies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information collected through our games. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Opt out of personalised advertising (see Section 4).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
